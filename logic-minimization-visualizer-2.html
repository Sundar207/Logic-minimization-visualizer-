Logic Minimization Visualizer for Data Scientists

Every classifier rule, feature filter, and decision boundary is a Boolean expression in disguise. This visualizer turns a raw expression into a truth table, a Karnaugh map, and a provably-equivalent minimized form — so you can see exactly how simplification works, one grouping at a time.
